## Purpose

This release aligns the companion artifact with the submitted EMSE manuscript.

No benchmark systems, campaign outcomes, oracle logic, scoring procedures, or reported empirical results were modified.

Changes are limited to documentation, terminology harmonisation, traceability improvements, and metadata updates.

## Reproducibility

The release preserves the frozen empirical campaign used in the manuscript:

- N = 240 campaign runs
- 12 FSM-Bench-20 systems
- 4 local Ollama models
- Frozen scoring exports
- Gold reference FSMs
- Behavioural oracle suites

All reported manuscript results remain reproducible from the archived records.
